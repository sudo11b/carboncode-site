About

CarbonCode is a one-person mobile studio building focused, well-crafted apps — starting on iPhone and Apple Watch, with Android on the roadmap.

Built nights and weekends by an Army infantry veteran and NDT inspector who codes — every app starts with a real problem worth solving and ships only when it earns its place on your home screen.

The same approach now goes into website rebuilds for small businesses: sites shaped around how the business actually runs, with features like class scheduling and online sign-up built to order.
