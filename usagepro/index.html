What it does

Dashboard

A window that shows every limit your plan reports: the current session, the weekly limits, and any per-model limits. Each one gets a percentage and a reset countdown, and the number moves from green to yellow to red as you get close.

If your account has extra usage spend or a prepaid credit balance, those show up too. The set of limits differs by plan, and UsagePro shows what claude.ai returns for yours.

Menu bar

Your session percentage sits next to the menu bar icon. Click it for the rest of your limits. Turn on threshold alerts and you get a local notification when a limit crosses the level you pick.

The Dock icon and the menu bar icon can each be shown or hidden in Settings, and the app can launch at login.

History

claude.ai only reports where you are right now. UsagePro saves a snapshot each time it refreshes and charts them on your Mac: usage over time, the weekly breakdown by product, and a projection of where you are heading before the next reset. The History tab also shows your current credit balance.

History starts empty on day one and fills in as the app runs. It records only while UsagePro is running.

How it handles your account

You sign in on claude.ai's own login page, shown in a window inside the app. UsagePro keeps the resulting session cookie in the macOS Keychain and sends it only to claude.ai. Your usage history is a local database on your Mac.

There is no account with CarbonCode, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no CarbonCode servers. The app runs in the App Sandbox with outgoing network access only, and it checks in with claude.ai on the refresh interval you choose, once a minute at the fastest. Signing out clears the Keychain item and claude.ai's cookies. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Independent, and dependent on claude.ai

UsagePro for Claude is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Claude is a trademark of Anthropic, PBC.

It reads your own usage from claude.ai's private endpoints, the same ones claude.ai's web app uses. Those are undocumented and can change without notice. If they do, UsagePro may stop working until it is updated.
