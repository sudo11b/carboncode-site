UsagePro for Claude

Your Claude usage limits in the menu bar, so you can see how much of the session and the week you have left without opening a browser tab.

Download on the Mac App Store Support

macOS 14 or later. Free to download with a 7-day trial, then a one-time $2.99 unlock. An independent Mac app by CarbonCode, not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

What it does

Dashboard

A window that shows every limit your plan reports: the current session, the weekly limits, and any per-model limits. Each one gets a percentage, a color-coded bar, the exact reset time, and an "at this rate" projection that tells you whether you will run out before it resets.

Cards can be reordered, hidden and resized. If your account has extra usage spend or a prepaid credit balance, those show up too. The set of limits differs by plan, and UsagePro shows what claude.ai returns for yours.

Menu bar

Your session percentage sits next to the menu bar icon. Click it for the rest of your limits without opening the window. Run it as a Dock app, a menu bar app, or both, and have it launch at login.

History

claude.ai only reports where you are right now. UsagePro saves a snapshot each time it refreshes and charts them on your Mac, with 24-hour, 7-day, 30-day and all-time views for each limit, plus the weekly breakdown by product.

History starts empty on day one and fills in as the app runs. It records only while UsagePro is running.

Alerts

Set one threshold and get a macOS notification when any limit crosses it.

How it handles your account

You sign in on claude.ai's own login page, shown in a window inside the app. UsagePro keeps the resulting session key in the macOS Keychain and sends it only to claude.ai. Your usage history is a local database on your Mac.

There is no account with CarbonCode, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no CarbonCode servers. The app runs in the App Sandbox with network access only to claude.ai, and it checks in on the refresh interval you choose, once a minute at the fastest. Signing out clears the Keychain item and claude.ai's cookies. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Independent, and dependent on claude.ai

UsagePro for Claude is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Claude is a trademark of Anthropic, PBC.

It reads your own usage from claude.ai's private endpoints, the same ones claude.ai's web app uses. Those are undocumented and can change without notice. If they do, UsagePro may stop working until it is updated.
