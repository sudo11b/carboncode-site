DarkRoom RT

A gamma radiography toolkit for the field. Shot times, source decay, boundaries and code limits for Ir-192 and Co-60, built by an NDT tech who started out in RT.

Download on the App Store Support

iPhone and Apple Watch. Iridium-192 and Cobalt-60 gamma sources; X-ray tubes are not covered.

Inside the app

What it does

Exposure

Shot time from source activity, steel-equivalent thickness, source-to-film distance and your crew's R factor, with the source decayed to today. The common slide-rule field formula means the R your crew already uses carries over. Learn R corrects it from a shot you trust.

Shot timer

Start the countdown straight from the result. DarkRoom asks to alert you when the shot ends, so you hear it with the phone locked. Saved shots go on today's list, which clears each day.

Calculators

Geometric unsharpness, minimum source-to-film distance, inverse square law, belt spacing, equivalent thickness, exclusion zone and IQI selection, plus a pipe chart.

Codes

Plain-language acceptance limits for API 1104, ASME B31.3, ASME Section V and AWS D1.1, worked out for the wall or weld size you enter.

Apple Watch

The shot on your wrist

The countdown runs on your watch, with a tap when the shot ends. Stop it from either device. Today's saved shots are there too, so you can see what you shot without pulling out the phone.

Your data

There is no account, no analytics and no CarbonCode server. Everything runs on your iPhone, with or without a signal. Your R factors, Γ values and boundary rates sync between your own devices through iCloud. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

A calculation aid, not a procedure

DarkRoom does not replace your training, your written procedure or a qualified Level II or Level III. Check results against a known shot, and confirm every boundary with a calibrated survey meter.

API 1104, ASME B31.3, ASME Section V and AWS D1.1 are published by the American Petroleum Institute, ASME and the American Welding Society. DarkRoom is not affiliated with or endorsed by them, and it does not reproduce their standards.
