What it does

Exposure

Shot time from source activity, steel-equivalent thickness, source-to-film distance and your crew's R factor, with the source decayed to today. The common slide-rule field formula means the R your crew already uses carries over. Learn R corrects it from a shot you trust.

Calculators

Geometric unsharpness, minimum source-to-film distance, inverse square law, belt spacing, equivalent thickness, exclusion zone and IQI selection, plus a pipe chart.

Codes

Plain-language acceptance limits for API 1104, ASME B31.3, ASME Section V and AWS D1.1, worked out for the wall or weld size you enter.

Apple Watch

The shot countdown on your wrist, with a tap when the shot ends. Stop it from either device.

Your data

There is no account, no analytics and no CarbonCode server. Everything runs on your iPhone. Your R factors, Γ values and boundary rates sync between your own devices through iCloud. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

A calculation aid, not a procedure

DarkRoom does not replace your training, your written procedure or a qualified Level II or Level III. Check results against a known shot, and confirm every boundary with a calibrated survey meter.
