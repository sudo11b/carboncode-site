1. The App

DarkRoom is a calculation aid for gamma radiographic testing with Iridium-192 and Cobalt-60 sources. It estimates shot times, source decay, boundary distances, geometric unsharpness and related values from published formulas and the values you enter, and it shows plain-language summaries of acceptance limits from industry codes.
