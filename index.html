New in 2.0 On the App Store

FormulaPulse

The F1 companion for race weekends. Version 2.0 adds Pit Wall: ask about pace, gaps, tyres and pit windows during a session, and get answers worked out from real timing data.

  • Racing news from more than a dozen sources in one feed
  • Spoiler Shield holds results until you have watched
  • Schedule, standings and circuit detail, plus Apple Watch

iPhone and Apple Watch. Free, with an optional Pro subscription.

UsagePro for Claude

Your Claude usage limits in the Mac menu bar and a dashboard window. See how much of the session and the week you have left, when each limit resets, and whether you will run out before it does.

  • Session percentage right next to the menu bar icon
  • History charts that stay on your Mac
  • Seven days free, then a one-time $2.99 unlock

macOS 14 or later. Independent app, not affiliated with Anthropic.

New On the App Store

DarkRoom RT

A gamma radiography toolkit for the field. Shot times, source decay, boundaries and code limits for Ir-192 and Co-60, built by an NDT tech who started out in RT.

  • Shot time and boundary distances from your crew's R factor
  • A shot timer on iPhone and Apple Watch
  • API 1104, ASME B31.3, ASME Section V and AWS D1.1 limits in plain words

iPhone and Apple Watch. A calculation aid, not a substitute for your procedure or a survey meter.
