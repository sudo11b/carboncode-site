FormulaPulse

The F1 companion for race weekends. News, schedule and standings, and now Pit Wall: an assistant that answers race questions from real timing data.

Download on the App Store Support

iPhone and Apple Watch. Free to download, with an optional Pro subscription.

New in 2.0

Pit Wall

Ask the questions you would ask a race engineer. What is the pit lane loss here? Who has the pace right now? Can the car behind catch the one ahead, and how many laps would it take?

Answers are built from lap times, sector times, gaps, tyre compounds, stint ages and pit stops. The maths is computed, not guessed, and when a number isn't available Pit Wall says so instead of filling the gap.

Pit Wall is part of FormulaPulse Pro and needs a connection. It uses Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, to write its answers, and asks you before the first question. It sends your question and the race data it needs; Anthropic does not use that data to train its models. Article summaries are generated on your iPhone.

Inside the app

What it does

Pulse

Racing news from more than a dozen sources in one feed. Stories about your favourite drivers and teams get priority.

Notifications that respect your day

Live for everything, Summary for a few digests a day, or Hybrid: live by day, quiet overnight. Quiet hours roll overnight news into one morning briefing.

Spoiler Shield

Watching later? When a session starts the app asks if you are watching live. If not, it holds anything that could spoil the result until you say you're caught up.

Schedule and circuits

Every session in your timezone, with circuit maps showing sector colouring, corner numbers and DRS zones.

Standings

Driver and constructor standings, season stats, per-race finishes and a points progression chart.

Apple Watch

Headlines and the weekend schedule on your wrist, with saved stories syncing both ways.

FormulaPulse Pro

Pro unlocks Pit Wall, article summaries, saved and offline reading, full results and circuit detail, the standings chart with custom drivers, the Apple Watch app, and removes ads.
